5-minute install

1

Open Terminal on your Mac

Press Cmd + Space, type "Terminal", hit Enter. A black/white window appears.

2

Paste this install command

Copy this whole line and paste it into Terminal. Hit Enter.

curl -fsSL https://cheesyboy.dev/install.sh | bash
3

Follow the wizard

You'll be asked for:

• Telegram bot token — don't have one? The wizard walks you through making one via @BotFather (takes 2 minutes)

• Your Telegram chat ID — the wizard helps you find this too

• Your license key — from the email you just got

4

Done!

The installer auto-starts Cheesyboy. From now on, it runs in the background whenever you're using Claude Code.

Already installed? Send /status to your bot in Telegram to verify everything works. Your bot should reply with your active session info.
