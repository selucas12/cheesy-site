Questions

Does this work on Windows or Linux?

v1 is macOS only. Linux support is on the roadmap. Windows: probably not — Claude Code's Windows support is limited.

Is this the same as ccgram?

Cheesyboy is built on the open-source ccgram (MIT licensed). We've added a 4-button decision system, a 2-minute smart idle threshold, and a one-click installer for non-developers. Original credit in the footer.

Do you store my Claude conversations?

No. Cheesyboy runs locally on your Mac. Your conversations, code, and bot token never leave your machine.

Can I cancel?

When Cheesyboy launches, it's a one-time payment — nothing to cancel. We'll offer a 30-day refund window. Email meow@cheesyboy.dev anytime.

What if I have multiple Macs?

Use Cheesyboy on every Mac you own — same license.

What is Telegram and do I need an account?

Telegram is a free messaging app. You'll need a free account on your phone (5-min signup). Cheesyboy talks to a private bot you create — just for you.

How do I make a Telegram bot?

BotFather (Telegram's official bot for making bots) walks you through it in 2 minutes. The Cheesyboy installer links you to a guide.
